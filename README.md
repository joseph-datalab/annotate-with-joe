## How I Work

Accuracy and consistency are important to me, especially when working with data that will be used to train or evaluate AI systems.

My approach includes:

- Reading and understanding project guidelines before starting
- Applying labels and annotations consistently
- Creating accurate annotation and segmentation boundaries
- Reviewing AI outputs against evaluation criteria
- Checking for missing, incorrect, or inconsistent data
- Identifying unclear, ambiguous, and edge cases
- Following project-specific quality standards
- Documenting or flagging uncertain cases when required
- Performing quality checks before submission
- Reviewing feedback and correcting errors
- Adapting to guideline updates and project changes

I would rather take the time to understand a difficult case than make a quick guess that could affect the quality of the dataset or evaluation.
